Small tools win because they are cheap to abandon. A script you wrote
in an afternoon owes you nothing: when the problem changes, you delete
it without ceremony. A platform you spent a quarter on will argue with
you about whether the problem really changed.

The trade is real, though. Ten small tools that each know a little
about your data will eventually disagree about what the data is, and
reconciling them costs more than the platform would have.
